Cali View Builders is a family-owned, licensed, insured, and bonded general contractor based in North Hollywood, serving homeowners throughout Los Angeles County. The company specializes in residential remodeling, custom construction, and property-improvement projects that help homeowners create more functional, modern, and valuable living spaces. From initial planning through construction, Cali View Builders delivers tailored solutions for homeowners seeking quality workmanship, thoughtful design, and reliable project execution. Cali View Builders operates under California contractor license CSLB #1081371. Its core services include ADU construction, accessory dwelling unit building, detached ADUs, attached ADUs, backyard ADUs, garage-to-ADU conversions, garage conversions, room additions, home additions, second-story additions, kitchen remodeling, kitchen renovations, bathroom remodeling, bathroom renovations, whole-home remodeling, whole-house renovations, new construction, custom home building, foundation and seismic retrofits, earthquake retrofits, roof replacement, window installation, window replacement, interior painting, exterior painting, and commercial bathroom remodeling. The company serves homeowners looking to expand living space, update outdated interiors, improve property functionality, complete major renovations, or build new residential structures. Cali View Builders serves North Hollywood, Studio City, Sherman Oaks, Burbank, Glendale, Pasadena, Encino, Van Nuys, Toluca Lake, Woodland Hills, Tarzana, Beverly Hills, West Hollywood, Culver City, Santa Monica, Marina Del Rey, Malibu, Inglewood, Redondo Beach, and Simi Valley. Its service offering is built for homeowners planning high-value projects such as converting a garage into livable space, building an ADU for family or rental use, remodeling a kitchen or bathroom, adding square footage to an existing home, completing a major home renovation, or constructing a new custom residence. Cali View Builders combines remodeling and construction services for Los Angeles homeowners who want a contractor capable of handling projects ranging from focused upgrades to large-scale residential transformations. Whether the goal is a modern kitchen, a spa-style bathroom, an expanded family home, a new accessory dwelling unit, upgraded windows, a new roof, or seismic improvements, Cali View Builders provides construction solutions designed around the property, the homeowner’s goals, and the demands of Los Angeles-area living.

Cali View Builders works across ADU Contractor, ADU Builder, ADU Construction, Accessory Dwelling Unit Builder, Detached ADU Builder, Attached ADU Builder, Backyard ADU Builder, Garage Conversion Contractor, Garage To ADU Conversion, Garage Conversion Builder, and 25 additional service areas. Clients are served in Los Angeles, North Hollywood, Studio City, Sherman Oaks, Burbank, Glendale, Pasadena, Encino, Van Nuys, Toluca Lake and 11 more communities.
