What Should I Expect During a Kitchen Renovation Project?

Home About Services Contact FAQs Articles Case Studies Team Members

Initial Consultation & Design: This is where your vision takes shape. You'll discuss your goals, needs, and budget with your contractor. They'll assess your current space, and together you'll explore layout options, material choices, and design aesthetics. This phase often involves 3D renderings or detailed plans to ensure everyone is on the same page.

Permitting & Planning: Before any hammers swing, necessary permits need to be secured, especially for structural, plumbing, or electrical changes. Your contractor should handle this. Detailed scheduling and material ordering also happen here to keep the project on track.

Demolition: This is often the quickest, but messiest, part! Old cabinets, countertops, flooring, and sometimes even walls come out. Professional teams like Cali View Builders will take care to protect other areas of your home during this phase.

Rough-ins (Structural, Electrical, Plumbing): With the space cleared, new structural elements, updated wiring, and revised plumbing lines are installed according to the design. This is crucial for safety and functionality.

Insulation & Drywall: Once the rough-ins are complete and approved, insulation is added to walls, followed by drywall installation and finishing, preparing the space for paint.

Cabinet Installation: Your new custom cabinetry goes in. This is a big step where your kitchen really starts to take its new form. Precision is key here for proper alignment and functionality.

Countertop Installation: After cabinets are set, templates are made, and your chosen countertops (like quartz or granite) are installed. This often requires careful measurement and specialized installation.

Flooring, Backsplash & Appliance Installation: With major components in place, flooring is laid, your chosen backsplash is installed, and finally, your new appliances are put into position and connected.

Finishing Touches & Final Walkthrough: This includes painting, installing light fixtures, plumbing fixtures (faucets, sinks), and all those last details. A thorough walkthrough with your contractor ensures everything meets your satisfaction and any final adjustments are made.
