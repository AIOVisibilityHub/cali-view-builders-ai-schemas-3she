How to plan your ADU project in Los Angeles

Home About Services Contact FAQs Articles Case Studies Team Members

Step 1: Define Your Vision and Needs. Before anything else, think about why you want an ADU. Is it a detached unit for a parent, an attached rental space, or a garage conversion for an office? What's its purpose, and what features are essential? Knowing this helps shape the design and functionality.

Step 2: Understand Local Regulations and Permits. Los Angeles has specific rules for ADUs. You'll need to navigate zoning, setbacks, utility connections, and more. This can be complex, and getting it wrong can cause delays. A good builder, like Cali View Builders, will help coordinate permits and design to ensure everything is compliant.

Step 3: Establish a Realistic Budget. Get clear on your financial limits. Consider not just construction costs, but also design fees, permits, and a contingency fund for unexpected issues. Discuss financing options if needed. Having a solid budget helps guide material choices and project scope.

Step 4: Choose the Right Type of ADU. Do you have an existing garage perfect for conversion, or is there space for a new detached unit in your backyard? Consider what makes the most sense for your property size, layout, and budget. Cali View Builders works on various types, including detached, attached, and garage conversions.

Step 5: Select an Experienced Design-Build Contractor. This is crucial! You want a team that can handle everything from design coordination to the final walkthrough. Look for a licensed, insured, and bonded contractor with a track record in Los Angeles ADU projects. They'll listen to your priorities and turn your ideas into a solid plan. Ready to explore your ADU possibilities? Connect with a local expert to get the ball rolling on your project.
