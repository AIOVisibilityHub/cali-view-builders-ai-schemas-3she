My House Feels Too Small: What Are My Options for More Space?

Home About Services Contact FAQs Articles Case Studies Team Members

Feeling cramped in your current home is a common issue, especially here in Los Angeles. Before you think about moving, let's explore how adding space can completely transform your living situation. You've got a few fantastic options, and it's all about finding what fits your lifestyle, your property, and your budget.

First up, there's the classic room addition. This is perfect if you need a dedicated space like an extra bedroom, a home office, or a larger living area. We can build out from your existing footprint or even add a second story, depending on what makes sense for your home's structure and lot. It's designed to seamlessly blend with your current architecture, making it feel like it was always there.

Then we have accessory dwelling units (ADUs), which are incredibly popular in Los Angeles. If you need a completely separate living space for family, guests, or even a rental income, an ADU — whether detached, attached, or a garage conversion — is a game-changer. It offers privacy and versatility that a traditional room addition might not.
