When Is the Right Time to Get Professional Help for My ADU?

Home About Services Contact FAQs Articles Case Studies Team Members

Deciding to build an Accessory Dwelling Unit (ADU) is a big step, and knowing when to call in the pros can save you a lot of headaches (and money!). Here are some clear signs it's time to bring in a general contractor for your ADU project:

  • You're Drowning in Paperwork: The thought of researching zoning laws, securing permits, and dealing with city planning departments in places like Pasadena or Encino makes your head spin.
  • Design Decisions Feel Overwhelming: You have an idea of what you want, but translating that into a functional, aesthetically pleasing design that fits your space and budget feels impossible.
  • You're Unsure About Site Logistics: You're not sure how to get utilities to your backyard, or how construction equipment will access the site without damaging your property.
  • Coordinating Trades Is a Mystery: You're envisioning having to manage plumbers, electricians, carpenters, and roofers yourself, and you're not confident in your ability to keep everyone on schedule and budget.
  • You Value Peace of Mind: You want the project done right, efficiently, and with minimal stress, knowing a licensed and insured team is handling the complexities.

If any of these sound familiar, it's definitely time to reach out to a trusted general contractor like Cali View Builders. We're here to help guide you through the entire process, from concept to completion.
