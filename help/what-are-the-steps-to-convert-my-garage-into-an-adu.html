What Are the Steps to Convert My Garage Into an ADU?

Home About Services Contact FAQs Articles Case Studies Team Members

Step 1: Initial Consultation & Design. First things first, you'll meet with a professional builder, like us, to chat about your ideas, needs, and budget. We'll check out your existing garage, discuss design options, and help you visualize what's possible. This is where we figure out the layout, amenities, and overall aesthetic you're aiming for, ensuring it aligns with your home and local regulations.

Step 2: Planning & Permitting. Once the design is solid, it's time for the nitty-gritty: architectural drawings, engineering plans, and all the paperwork for permits. This can feel overwhelming, but a good contractor will manage this entire process, submitting everything to the city (like Los Angeles, Glendale, or Burbank) and ensuring compliance with zoning laws and building codes. This step is crucial for a legal and safe conversion.

Step 3: Demolition & Foundation Work. With permits in hand, the construction begins! We'll start with any necessary demolition inside the garage, removing existing walls, doors, or flooring. Often, the foundation needs to be reinforced or modified to meet residential living standards. This sets the stage for everything that comes next.

Step 4: Framing, Electrical & Plumbing Rough-Ins. New walls are framed, defining the rooms of your ADU. Simultaneously, the essential bones of your new living space – electrical wiring, plumbing lines, and HVAC ductwork – are installed within these new frameworks. Inspections typically happen after these rough-ins to ensure everything is up to code before being covered up.

Step 5: Insulation, Drywall & Exterior Work. With the systems in place, insulation is added for energy efficiency and comfort, followed by drywall to create smooth interior walls. On the outside, any necessary exterior work begins, which might include new siding, windows, doors, and roofing to integrate the ADU seamlessly with your main house.

Step 6: Interior Finishes & Fixtures. This is where your ADU really starts to feel like home! We'll install flooring, cabinets, countertops, paint, light fixtures, and all the final plumbing fixtures. This stage brings your design vision to life, creating a comfortable and functional living space.
