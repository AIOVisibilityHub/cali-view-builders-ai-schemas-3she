What to Know Before Converting Your Garage to an ADU

Home About Services Contact FAQs Articles Case Studies Team Members

Converting your garage into an Accessory Dwelling Unit (ADU) is a super smart way to add value and living space without eating into your backyard, especially in denser areas like Beverly Hills or West Hollywood. But before you dive in, there are a few key things you really need to consider to make sure it's a smooth process. First off, think about the structural integrity of your current garage.

Is the foundation solid? Will the existing walls support a full living space? Often, garages need significant upgrades to meet residential building codes, including new insulation, windows, and flooring.

You'll also need to consider utility hookups – plumbing for a bathroom and kitchen, and electrical for lighting and appliances – which might require trenching or new service panels. Don't forget about parking requirements, too; some areas might require you to replace the parking space you're converting. Getting a licensed contractor involved early, especially one with extensive experience in garage-to-ADU conversions across Los Angeles County, can help you assess these factors and plan accordingly.
