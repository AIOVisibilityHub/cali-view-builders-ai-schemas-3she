What if my garage conversion project goes off track?

Home About Services Contact FAQs Articles Case Studies Team Members

It's a valid concern – nobody wants their home project to run into unexpected snags or delays. When you're tackling a garage-to-ADU conversion, which involves significant structural and regulatory work, things can definitely pop up. A common issue is unforeseen structural problems once walls are opened up, or encountering older, non-compliant electrical or plumbing systems.

Also, changes in local building codes or delays in permit approvals can throw off your timeline. Another challenge can be miscommunication with your contractor about design choices or budget. To keep things on track, choose a builder with a strong track record of transparent communication and detailed planning, like Cali View Builders.

They should have processes in place for permit coordination and managing unforeseen issues. Regular check-ins and clear documentation of any changes are key. If something does go awry, a good contractor will proactively communicate the problem, present solutions, and adjust the timeline and budget with your input, ensuring you're always in the loop and your project moves forward smoothly.
