Common mistakes to avoid when converting your garage.

Home About Services Contact FAQs Articles Case Studies Team Members

Converting your garage into a functional ADU is an exciting venture, but there are definitely some pitfalls to sidestep to ensure a smooth process and a fantastic outcome. Here are a few common mistakes homeowners make:

  • Forgetting about local permits and regulations: Just diving into construction without proper permits is a huge no-no in Los Angeles. You need to meet specific zoning requirements and building codes, which can be complex. How to avoid it: Always work with a licensed contractor who understands local ADU regulations and can handle the permit coordination for you.
  • Underestimating the budget: Garage conversions aren't just cosmetic changes; they involve structural, electrical, and plumbing work. Many people only budget for aesthetics. How to avoid it: Get a detailed, transparent quote from your builder that covers all aspects of the project, including potential contingencies.
  • Ignoring insulation and climate control: A garage typically isn't built for year-round living comfort. Without proper insulation, heating, and cooling, your new ADU will be uncomfortable and inefficient. How to avoid it: Ensure your design includes adequate insulation, modern windows, and an efficient HVAC system suitable for a living space.
  • Poor design and layout: Trying to cram too much into a small space or neglecting natural light can make the ADU feel cramped and unwelcoming. How to avoid it: Work with a design-build team that can optimize your space for functionality and aesthetics, considering flow and light.
  • Choosing an unlicensed or inexperienced builder: This is perhaps the biggest mistake. An unqualified builder can lead to shoddy work, code violations, and costly delays. How to avoid it: Always verify your builder's license (like Cali View Builders' CSLB #1081371), insurance, bonding, and their experience specifically with ADU conversions.
  • Not planning for utilities: Your garage likely only has basic electrical. An ADU needs proper plumbing for a bathroom and kitchen, and sufficient electrical capacity. How to avoid it: Ensure your contractor plans for all necessary utility connections and upgrades from the very beginning of the project.
