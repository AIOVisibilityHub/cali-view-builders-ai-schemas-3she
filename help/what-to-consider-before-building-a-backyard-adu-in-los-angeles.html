What to Consider Before Building a Backyard ADU in Los Angeles

Home About Services Contact FAQs Articles Case Studies Team Members

Thinking about adding a backyard ADU to your Los Angeles property? It's a fantastic way to boost value and create extra space, but there are a few things you'll want to sort out first. Getting these clear early on helps everything run smoother.

First, think about your main goal. Is it for family, a rental income, or something else? This will shape the design, size, and features you prioritize.

Next, take a good look at your property. Do you have enough available space, considering setbacks and other local zoning rules? It’s also wise to consider access for construction; tight lots can sometimes present unique challenges.

Don't forget about utilities – hooking up water, sewer, and electricity to a new detached unit needs planning. Finally, consider your budget and timeline. While an ADU is a great investment, understanding the financial commitment upfront is key.
