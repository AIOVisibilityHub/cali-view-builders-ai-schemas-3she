When should I bring in a pro for my garage conversion?

Home About Services Contact FAQs Articles Case Studies Team Members

Thinking about turning that dusty old garage into a super functional ADU? That's a smart move for homeowners in Los Angeles looking to expand their space or boost property value. While the idea might start with a Pinterest board, knowing when to bring in a professional general contractor can save you a ton of stress, time, and money in the long run. It's often sooner than you think!

Here are some clear signs it’s time to call in a professional

  • You're feeling overwhelmed by permits: Los Angeles County has specific and often complex regulations for ADUs and garage conversions. If you're finding the permit process daunting or confusing, a pro can handle all the red tape.
  • Structural questions are popping up: Is your garage foundation solid? Does the roof need reinforcing? Are the walls up to code for living space? If you're not 100% sure about the structural integrity, an expert assessment is crucial.
  • You need design ideas and functionality: Beyond just walls, you want a space that's genuinely livable and looks great. A contractor with design-build experience can help you visualize and create the best layout for your new ADU.
  • Managing multiple trades sounds like a nightmare: Converting a garage involves plumbers, electricians, framers, drywallers, and more. If coordinating all these folks and their schedules gives you a headache, a general contractor steps in to manage the entire project seamlessly.
  • Budgeting and timeline feel like a guessing game: An experienced builder can provide a detailed, realistic estimate and project schedule, helping you avoid unexpected costs and delays.
