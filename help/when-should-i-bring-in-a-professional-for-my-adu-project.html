When should I bring in a professional for my ADU project?

Home About Services Contact FAQs Articles Case Studies Team Members

Building an Accessory Dwelling Unit (ADU) is an exciting venture, but it's also a significant construction project. While it's tempting to try and manage some parts yourself, there are definite signs that bringing in a professional general contractor will save you time, stress, and potentially money in the long run. Cali View Builders has been helping Los Angeles homeowners with these projects since 2013, and we've seen it all!

  • When you're overwhelmed by regulations: Los Angeles County has specific zoning laws, building codes, and permitting requirements for ADUs. If the thought of navigating these feels like too much, a professional contractor specializing in ADUs can handle the permit coordination for you. They know the ins and outs, ensuring your project complies from day one.
  • When your vision feels too complex: If you have a specific design in mind that involves intricate layouts, unique materials, or integrating new structures with existing ones (like a garage conversion), a professional design-build team can turn those ideas into a practical, buildable plan. They have the expertise to make challenging visions a reality.
  • When you need reliable project management: An ADU project involves multiple trades, scheduling, material procurement, and inspections. If you don't have the time or experience to manage all these moving parts yourself, a general contractor provides comprehensive coordination. They're your single point of contact, keeping the project on track and within scope.
  • When you want quality craftsmanship and durability: Building an ADU is an investment in your property. You want it built right, to last. A professional builder ensures quality workmanship, proper waterproofing, and structural integrity. They stand behind their work, giving you confidence in your new living space.
  • When you're looking for tailored solutions: Every home and homeowner is unique. If you want an ADU that's truly designed around your property and your specific goals—whether it's for rental income in Glendale or a family suite in Beverly Hills—a professional builder will offer customized solutions rather than one-size-fits-all approaches. They listen first to understand your needs.
