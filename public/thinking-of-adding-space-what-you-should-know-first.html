Thinking of Adding Space? What You Should Know First

Home About Services Contact FAQs Articles Case Studies Team Members

Dreaming of a bigger home? Home additions can totally transform your living space, giving you that extra room you've always wanted. But before you jump in, there are a few key things to consider to make sure your project goes smoothly. First off, think about what you really need. Is it another bedroom, a home office, or a more expansive living area? Getting super clear on the function helps guide the design.

Next, consider how the new space will integrate with your existing home. You want it to feel like it's always been there, not like an afterthought. That means matching styles, materials, and even flow. Permitting is another big one; in Los Angeles County, there are specific requirements, and getting this right from the start is crucial. It’s also wise to think about your long-term goals for the property. An addition can significantly increase your home’s value, so planning for a solid return on investment is smart.
