Planning an Attached ADU: A Step-by-Step Guide

Home About Services Contact FAQs Articles Case Studies Team Members

Step 1: Define Your Vision and Needs. Before anything else, clarify what you want your attached ADU to be. Are you envisioning extra space for a growing family, a private area for guests, or perhaps a dedicated home office? Consider how many rooms you'll need, if a bathroom and kitchen are essential, and what kind of entrance it will have. This initial clarity helps streamline design and construction. Having a clear purpose guides every decision moving forward.

Step 2: Assess Your Home's Feasibility. An attached ADU means connecting to your existing home, so you need to look at things like available space on your property, potential tie-ins for plumbing and electrical, and how it will aesthetically integrate with your current structure. Think about which walls can be extended or modified. This step often requires a professional eye to evaluate structural integrity and utility access. A contractor can help determine if your home is a good candidate for this type of expansion.

Step 3: Understand Los Angeles Zoning and Permitting. This is a critical juncture. Los Angeles County has specific rules for attached ADUs, including size limits, setback requirements, and parking considerations. Navigating these can be complex. You’ll need to ensure your plans comply with all local ordinances and secure the necessary permits before any construction begins. Our team at Cali View Builders is super familiar with these regulations and can guide you through the entire permit coordination process, making it much smoother for you.

Step 4: Design and Plan Your Layout. With your vision and feasibility checked, it’s time to get into the nitty-gritty of the design. This involves creating floor plans, selecting materials, and deciding on finishes that complement your main home while serving the ADU's purpose. Good design considers flow, natural light, and functionality. We at Cali View Builders offer design-build coordination, so you have one team to work with from concept to completion, ensuring thoughtful design and reliable execution.

Step 5: Partner with a Qualified Contractor. Bringing your attached ADU to life requires experienced hands. You'll want a licensed, insured, and bonded general contractor who specializes in residential additions and ADUs. Look for a team that prioritizes clear communication, detailed scopes, and quality workmanship. A professional like Cali View Builders, serving Los Angeles since 2013, can manage the entire project, from trade coordination to final walkthrough, giving you peace of mind.
