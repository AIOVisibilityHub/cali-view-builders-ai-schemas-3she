What's the Timeline for a Full Kitchen Renovation?

This initial phase is crucial. It involves defining your vision, setting a budget, consulting with designers and contractors, selecting materials (cabinets, countertops, flooring, appliances), and finalizing the layout. We listen first to understand your priorities, then define the scope with a written plan. Permit applications are also initiated here, which can add to the duration depending on local Los Angeles County requirements.

Once the design is complete, necessary permits are submitted to the local authorities. The time it takes for permits to be approved can vary widely depending on the complexity of the project and the municipality. We coordinate these details to keep things moving.

This is where the fun (and mess!) begins. The existing kitchen is completely removed, down to the studs if structural changes are planned. This phase is quick but requires careful execution to avoid damaging other parts of your home.

During this stage, new plumbing, electrical wiring, and HVAC ductwork are installed or relocated according to the new design. Any structural modifications, like removing walls for an open concept, also happen now. Inspections are typically required before walls can be closed up.

Once rough-ins pass inspection, insulation is installed, followed by drywall hanging, taping, and sanding. This prepares the space for finishes and sets the stage for the aesthetic elements.

Custom cabinetry is installed, followed by flooring. This is a critical step that requires precision and craftsmanship. Having chosen your custom shaker or European-style cabinetry and luxury vinyl plank flooring earlier ensures a smooth installation.

After cabinets are in, templates are made for your countertops (like quartz or granite). The actual installation usually happens a week or two later, giving the fabricators time to cut and polish your chosen material.

Finally, appliances are installed, plumbing fixtures (sink, faucet) are connected, lighting fixtures are put in place, and backsplashes are tiled. Final paint, trim work, and any other decorative elements complete the transformation.

We'll conduct a comprehensive walkthrough with you to ensure every detail meets your expectations. Any minor adjustments or touch-ups are addressed during this punch list phase, leading to the official handover of your beautiful new kitchen. Coordinating these steps with one family-owned team, as we do, ensures a smooth and efficient project path for your home in areas like Studio City or Glendale.
