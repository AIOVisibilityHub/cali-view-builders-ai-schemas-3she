Maximizing Space with a Detached ADU

Home About Services Contact FAQs Articles Case Studies Team Members

When you're thinking about adding a detached Accessory Dwelling Unit (ADU) to your property in areas like Marina Del Rey or Malibu, you're looking at a fantastic opportunity to really maximize your outdoor space. A detached ADU gives you ultimate flexibility in design and privacy, making it feel like a completely separate home. But getting the most out of it means thinking smart about the layout and how it interacts with your main house and yard.

Consider things like natural light – orienting the ADU to get plenty of sun can make it feel much larger and more inviting. Think about outdoor living areas, too; a small patio or deck can extend the usable space dramatically. Efficient interior design, like built-in storage and multi-functional furniture, is also key to making every square foot count.

Don't forget about utility connections and maintaining privacy for both your main home and the ADU. A seasoned ADU builder can help you creatively plan the placement and design to ensure your detached unit perfectly complements your property and meets all your needs.
