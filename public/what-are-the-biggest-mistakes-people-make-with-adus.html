What are the biggest mistakes people make with ADUs?

Home About Services Contact FAQs Articles Case Studies Team Members

Building an Accessory Dwelling Unit (ADU) can be an awesome way to add value and space to your property, but it's easy to stumble if you're not careful. One big mistake folks make is ignoring local zoning and permit requirements. Los Angeles County has specific rules, and trying to skirt them will only lead to delays and headaches.

Make sure you're working with a team that knows the ins and outs of Beverly Hills, Burbank, or wherever you are in our service area. Another common pitfall is underestimating the project's true cost, often by not planning for unexpected issues or finishing touches. A solid general contractor like Cali View Builders can help you set a realistic budget from the get-go.

Many homeowners also make the mistake of skimping on design. An ADU isn't just a shed; it's a living space! Thoughtful design ensures it blends with your existing home and maximizes functionality, whether it's a garage-to-ADU conversion or a brand-new build.

Lastly, picking the wrong contractor can derail everything. You want a licensed, insured, and bonded team that communicates clearly and has a proven track record, especially in areas like Santa Monica or Glendale. Don't rush this decision; it's crucial for a smooth project.

Getting it right means your ADU can be a fantastic asset for years to come. Think of it as investing in an extension of your lifestyle.
