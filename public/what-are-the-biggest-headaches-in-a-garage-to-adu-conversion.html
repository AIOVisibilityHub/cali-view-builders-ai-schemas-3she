What are the biggest headaches in a garage to ADU conversion?

Home About Services Contact FAQs Articles Case Studies Team Members

Turning your garage into an Accessory Dwelling Unit (ADU) can be a fantastic way to add value and space, but it's not without its challenges. Folks often run into unexpected issues that can really slow things down or blow the budget if they're not prepared. Things like tricky zoning laws, structural surprises, or even just finding reliable contractors can turn an exciting project into a stressful one. Getting ahead of these potential problems is key to a smooth conversion process.

One common snag is navigating Los Angeles County's specific zoning and permitting requirements. These can be pretty complex and vary even within neighborhoods, so what worked for your neighbor might not work for you. It's smart to dive deep into these rules early on.

Another big one is dealing with the existing garage structure. Sometimes, what looks solid from the outside might need significant upgrades to meet residential building codes, like foundation work or roof reinforcement. Skipping this assessment can lead to costly rework later.

Then there's the coordination of different trades – electricians, plumbers, framers, you name it. If you're trying to manage everyone yourself, keeping the project on schedule and ensuring quality can be a huge headache. A good general contractor acts as your single point of contact, streamlining this whole process.

Finally, managing the budget and timeline effectively can be tough. Unexpected delays or material costs can quickly add up. Having a detailed plan and a contractor who provides clear scopes helps keep everything on track.
