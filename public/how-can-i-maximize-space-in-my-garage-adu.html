How can I maximize space in my garage ADU?

Home About Services Contact FAQs Articles Case Studies Team Members

Garages often feel small, but with smart planning, you can create a surprisingly spacious ADU! Maximizing every inch is key, especially in busy Los Angeles areas like West Hollywood or Santa Monica. First off, think vertically.

High ceilings in garages are common, so consider adding built-in storage or even a small loft for a sleeping area or extra storage. This draws the eye upward and makes the space feel larger. Second, choose multi-functional furniture.

A sofa bed, a fold-down table, or shelving units that double as room dividers can save valuable floor space. Every piece should serve a purpose, and ideally, more than one! Third, opt for clever storage solutions.

Built-in cabinetry, wall-mounted shelves, and even under-bed storage can keep clutter at bay and make the area feel open. Don't forget recessed lighting too; it keeps the ceiling clean and maximizes headroom. Fourth, strategic use of natural light and light colors can make a huge difference.

Large windows, skylights, and light-colored paint on walls and ceilings reflect light and visually expand the space. Finally, work with a design-build team that specializes in ADUs. They'll have creative ideas for layouts, storage, and material choices that fit your specific garage dimensions and your lifestyle needs.
