How to get started with a house addition project

Home About Services Contact FAQs Articles Case Studies Team Members

Step 1: Define Your Needs and Vision. Before anything else, sit down and really think about why you need this addition and what you hope to achieve. Do you need an extra bedroom, a larger living space, a new home office, or maybe a dedicated hobby room? Consider how this new space will integrate with your existing home's flow and design. Gather inspiration from magazines, websites, or even other homes you admire. This initial clarity helps immensely when communicating with professionals.

Step 2: Research Local Regulations and Permitting. Los Angeles County has specific requirements for construction projects. Before you get too far, it's wise to understand the basics of what's allowed in your area, especially concerning setbacks, height restrictions, and zoning. A good general contractor will handle the permit coordination, but having a general idea yourself can be helpful. This ensures your project stays on track and complies with all local rules.

Step 3: Consult with a Design-Build Contractor. This is where the rubber meets the road. Look for a licensed, insured, and bonded general contractor, like Cali View Builders, who specializes in residential additions and offers design-build services. This means they can help you with both the design and the construction, providing a single point of contact. They'll listen to your ideas, assess your property's feasibility, and offer expert advice on how to best achieve your goals within your budget. They'll also help clarify the scope of work and answer any questions about the process.

Step 4: Develop a Detailed Project Scope and Budget. Once you've consulted with your contractor, they'll help you refine your vision into a concrete plan. This includes detailed architectural drawings, material selections, and a clear construction timeline. A comprehensive scope prevents misunderstandings and unexpected costs down the line. It's important to have a realistic budget in mind and discuss it openly with your contractor. Remember, investing in quality now often saves money in the long run.

Step 5: Prepare for Construction and Project Management. With plans approved and permits secured, it’s time for construction! Your chosen contractor will manage all the trades, coordinate inspections, and ensure the project progresses smoothly. They should keep you updated regularly on the progress and any decisions that need to be made. Being responsive and communicating clearly with your project manager during this phase will help keep everything on schedule and ensure the final result is exactly what you envisioned.
