Is a detached ADU right for my property?

Home About Services Contact FAQs Articles Case Studies Team Members

Thinking about adding a detached ADU to your Los Angeles property? It's a fantastic way to boost property value, create rental income, or provide extra space for family. But before you jump in, it's smart to consider if your property is the perfect fit. Cali View Builders works with homeowners all over LA, from North Hollywood to Beverly Hills, to figure this out.

  • Sufficient backyard space — Is there enough room in your yard to comfortably build a separate structure without feeling cramped? You'll need to account for the ADU footprint plus required setbacks from property lines and your main home. This ensures privacy and compliance with local codes.
  • Easy utility access — Can you easily connect to existing water, sewer, and electrical lines? Proximity to these utilities can significantly impact the cost and complexity of the project. This is a crucial factor for budget planning.
  • Clear access for construction — Can construction crews and equipment easily access the building site without damaging existing landscaping or structures? Good access makes the construction process smoother and more efficient.
  • Favorable zoning and regulations — Are there any specific zoning overlays or local regulations in your area that might affect a detached ADU? While state laws are generally supportive, local specifics can vary. Understanding these early avoids delays.
  • Long-term goals align — Does adding a detached ADU fit with your long-term plans for your property, whether it's for personal use, family, or as an investment? This ensures your investment serves your future needs.

How to use this checklist: Go through each item and honestly assess your property. If you can confidently check off most of these, a detached ADU is likely a great option for you. If you have questions about any of these points, especially regarding zoning or utility access, it's always best to chat with an experienced general contractor.
