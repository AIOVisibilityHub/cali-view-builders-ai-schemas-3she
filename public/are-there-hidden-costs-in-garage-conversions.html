Are there hidden costs in garage conversions?

Home About Services Contact FAQs Articles Case Studies Team Members

Jumping into a garage conversion project in Los Angeles is an exciting venture, promising more space or even a new income stream. But many homeowners wonder about the real cost, especially if there are unexpected expenses lurking. The truth is, while a good builder provides a detailed estimate, some 'hidden' costs can pop up if you're not working with an experienced team who knows what to look for upfront. It's all about being prepared and having a clear understanding of the full scope.

One common area for hidden costs is unexpected structural issues. Your existing garage might need foundation repairs, wall reinforcement, or roof upgrades to meet residential codes, which aren't always visible until demolition starts. A thorough initial inspection by a pro can often flag these.

Another big one is permit fees and regulatory compliance. Los Angeles County has specific requirements for ADUs, and the fees for permits, plans, and inspections can add up. An experienced builder will factor these into your initial proposal or at least clearly outline them.

Utility connections can also be a surprise. Depending on your current setup, extending plumbing, electrical, and HVAC to the new ADU can be more complex and costly than anticipated, especially if trenching or major panel upgrades are needed.

Then there's site access and logistics. If your garage is hard to reach for materials and equipment, or if there are tight property lines, these factors can increase labor costs.

Lastly, design changes or upgraded finishes during construction can quickly inflate the budget. While it's great to personalize, every change order has a cost. Having a clear design plan from the outset helps manage this.
