How to Plan Your Backyard ADU Project Smoothly

Home About Services Contact FAQs Articles Case Studies Team Members

Step 1: Define Your ADU Goals and Budget. What will your ADU be used for? How many bedrooms and bathrooms do you need? Having a clear vision will guide all subsequent decisions. Also, establish a realistic budget, including design, permits, and construction, to ensure your project stays on track financially.

Step 2: Check Local Zoning and Permit Requirements. Los Angeles County has specific rules for ADUs. Understand setbacks, height limits, and utility connections early on. A good general contractor often assists with this initial research, ensuring your design complies with all local regulations before you even start the design phase.

Step 3: Develop a Comprehensive Design. Work with designers to create a functional and aesthetically pleasing layout that maximizes your space and complements your existing home. This includes detailed floor plans, exterior renderings, and material selections. Remember, a well-thought-out design saves time and money during construction.

Step 4: Coordinate Engineering and Permitting. Once the design is finalized, engineering plans are needed to ensure structural integrity. This is often followed by the permit application process. Partnering with a contractor who handles design-build coordination can significantly streamline this, as they manage all submissions and communications with local authorities like those in Burbank or Beverly Hills.

Step 5: Select a Qualified Contractor. Choose a licensed, insured, and bonded general contractor with a strong track record in ADU construction, especially in your area of Los Angeles. They should offer clear communication, detailed scopes, and a transparent process from start to finish.
