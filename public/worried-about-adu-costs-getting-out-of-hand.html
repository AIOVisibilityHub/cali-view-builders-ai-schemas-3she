Worried about ADU costs getting out of hand?

Home About Services Contact FAQs Articles Case Studies Team Members

Building an Accessory Dwelling Unit (ADU) in Los Angeles can feel like a big financial commitment, and it's totally normal to be concerned about the budget. Many homeowners worry about hidden fees or unexpected expenses derailing their project, but with the right approach, you can keep your ADU build on track and within your means. The key is clear communication and detailed planning from the very beginning. You want to make sure your contractor is transparent about all costs involved.

First off, get a clear, detailed scope of work. This isn't just a basic quote; it should break down every aspect of the project, from design and permitting to materials and labor. A thorough scope helps prevent surprises down the line. Cali View Builders, for example, really emphasizes defining the scope early on so everyone's on the same page.

Next, understand what's included in your contract. Will it cover permit coordination, engineering, and design? Some contractors bundle these, while others list them separately. Knowing this upfront helps you compare apples to apples when getting bids.

Consider the impact of your material choices. While high-end finishes look great, they can quickly inflate your budget. Talk to your builder about cost-effective alternatives that still achieve the look and durability you want. Sometimes a small tweak can make a big difference.

Finally, always build in a contingency fund. Even with the best planning, construction projects can encounter unforeseen issues, especially with older homes or unique property conditions. Having a 10-15% buffer can save you a lot of stress.
